## Persistent BF16-PV SwiGLU ownership

PV projection partitions `n_ff / 8` groups among workers. The activation
previously partitioned individual rows with quotient/remainder distribution,
without a barrier after gate/up. With 4352 rows and 48 workers, worker 0
produces rows [0,88) but the old activation consumes [0,91). It can therefore
read another worker's unfinished results. The activation now consumes exactly
its producer's eight-row groups, retaining the scalar SiLU arithmetic and
avoiding an additional barrier.

`common/test_swiglu_pv.c` runs the real PV projection and activation helpers
with deliberately skewed worker execution. All 20 combinations of row count
and team size pass, including fewer groups than workers and 4352 rows/48
workers. Build/run command is in the test source. Production build:

```sh
make -C a64fx/llm tp_runner CC=fcc OPENMP=1
```

Run-to-run variation is significant; do not interpret the best row as a
statistically established speedup. Keep prefetch distance 6. The cadence probe
and slower full-head register PV experiment were removed. Fujitsu compilation
with `-msve-vector-bits=512` is unsupported by its SVE ACLE header. LLVM build:

```sh
make -C a64fx/llm tp_runner CC=clang OPENMP=1 \
  BUILD="$PWD/tmp/qwen38-clang21" \
  EXTRA_CFLAGS='-mcpu=a64fx -msve-vector-bits=512 -ffast-math -fno-finite-math-only'
```

`TF_ATTN_PV_SPLIT=1` divides output dimensions across the QK workers, preserving
each dimension's increasing-position FMA order. Softmax completion and PV
completion barriers protect score reads and the following gated attention.
`common/test_attn_pv_slice.c` passes 340 bitwise comparisons, including uneven
partitions and reversed worker order. `TP_DECODE_FUSED_HEAD=1` computes logits
inside the persistent trunk dispatch. Its vocabulary bound uses local
`output.n_rows`, and the argmax scan follows BF16-PV producer ownership.
Both features remain opt-in. `TF_BARRIER_BUSY_WAIT=1` additionally selects
busy waiting with the AArch64 YIELD hint instead of SEV/WFE sleeping in the
flat barrier (`TF_HIER_BARRIER=0`). This trades idle power for lower wake-up
latency; it is not enabled by default. Its two runs gave 28.10 and 28.35 tok/s,
with rank-0 communication at 2.60 ms/token in both. The WFE control was unusually
slow; do not use that outlier alone to calculate a speedup. The earlier uncached
baseline was 24.20 tok/s. Keep 48 threads and prefetch distance 6.
`TP_POOL_CORE_STRIPED=1` is available to spread smaller teams across all four
CMGs; 36 threads reduced communication but lost more in compute throughput.
SVE softmax is approximate, even though this
128-token greedy continuation matches; no floating-point exactness claim is made.
